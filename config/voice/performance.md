# Performance direction (TTS performance mode)

This block is sent as the PERFORMANCE section of the single-call TTS prompt (`reelsmith tts --mode=performance`). Per-video CONTEXT comes from the `## Voice direction` section of `script.md`; the TRANSCRIPT is the script's spoken lines. Override the file per video with `tts_performance: <path>` in the frontmatter.

PERFORMANCE

Style: Energetic, conversational, and sharp, with the confidence of someone explaining a major tech development to a curious audience. The opening should immediately grab attention, then move naturally through the points without sounding like a corporate presentation. Emphasize the key phrases of each script and the final shift or takeaway at the end.

Pace: Fast and engaging in the opening. Keep the middle section moving briskly as each point is introduced, with brief pauses after major points so they land clearly. Slow down slightly toward the ending as the bigger implication becomes clear, giving the final line enough space to resonate.

Accent: Natural South Asian English, clear, confident, and conversational. Avoid an exaggerated presenter or Americanized accent.
