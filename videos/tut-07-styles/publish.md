## Title
Style packs: same script, three looks

## Description
A style pack is a folder: STYLE.md, an optional kit.jsx and a reference video. Reelsmith ships reflective, tech-news and motion. Pick one in the frontmatter or the config, or copy a pack to make your own. Episode 7 of 10.

Reelsmith is open source: https://github.com/Aabishkar2/reelsmith
