# Publish: tut-05-voice

## Title
Voice: TTS or your own take

## Description
Sentence mode caches one clip per line; performance mode sends the whole script with a direction prompt (the voice in this video). Or record your own take with the teleprompter, analyze, re-record flagged lines, cut. Episode 5 of 10.

Reelsmith is open source: https://github.com/Aabishkar2/reelsmith
