# Publish: tut-06-animation

## Title
How Reelsmith keeps animation in sync

## Description
One HTML file, React and a small runtime. useWordCue binds every reveal to the second a word is spoken, every frame is a pure function of time, SubtitleRail gives free karaoke subtitles, and two linters guard it all. Episode 6 of 10.

Reelsmith is open source: https://github.com/Aabishkar2/reelsmith
