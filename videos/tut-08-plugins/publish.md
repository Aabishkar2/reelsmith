# Publish: tut-08-plugins

## Title
Plugins: bring your own TTS, STT, style, publish

## Description
Four plugin kinds, one small contract: a plugin.js exporting a name, a kind and a couple of functions. Register it in reelsmith.config.json or drop it in plugins/. Built in: OpenRouter TTS, local Whisper, three styles, YouTube, Meta and Discord. Episode 8 of 10.

Reelsmith is open source: https://github.com/Aabishkar2/reelsmith
